BP Notebook — Privacy Policy

BP Notebook collects nothing about you. Your blood pressure readings stay on your iPhone.

Last updated: September 2, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no third-party SDK that gathers data. The developer never receives your blood pressure values, pulse, measurement times, name, device information or location. There is no account to create.

Where your data lives

Readings, names and settings are stored only in a database on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Nothing is stored in iCloud. Blood pressure readings are personal health information, so the app is designed to keep them on the device. As a result there is no automatic sync between devices. Before changing phones, export your records from the export screen and keep the file.

Sharing with family

Enabling sharing never sends anything by itself. When a high reading appears and you tap the button, the Messages compose sheet opens on your iPhone. You choose the recipient and you send it. The message contains only the values and the time, and it never passes through a server of ours.

Sheets and exported files

The PDF sheet for your doctor and the CSV export are generated on your device. They are never sent anywhere automatically; you choose where to print, save or send them each time.

Reminders (notifications)

Measurement reminders are scheduled entirely on your device. We do not use push notifications from a server. Reminders never contain your blood pressure numbers, so nobody can read them on your lock screen.

Apple Health

If you turn on Health sync, the app writes your blood pressure and pulse to Apple Health and reads blood pressure records from Health into the notebook, using Apple's HealthKit on the device. Health data is used only to display and export your diary. It is never used for advertising or shared with anyone, and it never leaves your device through this app. Sync is off by default and only the device owner's profile can use it.

Purchases

Payments for "BP Notebook Plus" are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple Account has purchased; no payment details reach the app or the developer.

Children

This is a general utility app. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. The reference ranges shown by the app do not apply to people under 18.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new text ships with the app and is posted on this page with a new date.
